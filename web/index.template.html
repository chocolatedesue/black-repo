§ 1

What happens inside one revolution

A circular orbit's shadow geometry collapses to a single parameter: the beta angle β, the angle between the Sun and the orbit plane. Everything else — season, local time, inclination, node position — acts only through β.

本节要点
一圈之内的光照/阴影时长只由一个量决定：太阳与轨道面的夹角 β。下面这张图是轨道面内的精确剖面—— 地球阴影在轨道平面上的截线是一个椭圆，β 越大椭圆越细长，当它不再与轨道圆相交时，卫星全程见光。
Figure 1 · interactive Cross-section of the orbit plane: the shadow ellipse and the eclipse arc
Earth's shadow, intersected with the orbit plane, is the ellipse x²sin²β + y² = R² with R = R⊕ + 90 km — semi-minor axis R across the Sun line and semi-major R/sin β along it. The satellite is eclipsed on the anti-sunward half of that ellipse. As β grows the ellipse stretches until its tip clears the orbit radius at β* = arcsin(R/r), and the eclipse disappears entirely. Durations shown are from the simulation, not from the drawing.
Figure 2 Illumination profile ν(u) around one revolution, 550 km
ν is the unocculted fraction of the solar disc. At full-orbit scale the transitions look vertical, because a penumbral crossing spans only about a degree of argument of latitude — Figure 3 resolves one. What this view does show is the quantity that matters: as β approaches β*, the eclipse arc narrows towards nothing rather than shallowing, so a satellite either takes the full outage or none of it.
Figure 3 Shadow entry, resolved in seconds
The penumbral ramp lasts — at 550 km, β = 0 — about — of the eclipse. Small, but it is the entire difference between a conical and a cylindrical shadow model, and it is where a binary flag misplaces energy.
§ 2

The governing curve

One curve per altitude reproduces every eclipse duration in this study. It is flat near β = 0, then falls off a cliff at β*.

本节要点
遮蔽时长对 β 在 0–40° 区间几乎不敏感（只掉 1–2 分钟），但在 β* 附近几度之内就从二十多分钟直接归零。 这意味着能量供给不是平滑变化的，而是有一个"悬崖"——星座设计与任务调度必须按这个非线性来做。

§ 3

What a year looks like

β is not a design constant. In a sun-synchronous orbit it breathes with the seasons around a local-time-fixed mean; in an ordinary LEO it sweeps its full range every beta cycle, dragging the eclipse duration with it.

本节要点
SSO 的 β 一年之内只在一个窄带里摆动（由 LTAN 决定中心值），所以能量供给稳定、可预测； 而 i = 53° 这类非太阳同步轨道的 β 会在 ±76° 之间来回扫，一年里有若干段完全无阴影期、也有满遮蔽期， 能量供给呈强周期性——这正是能耗调度要建模的对象。

§ 4

The sun-synchronous design grid

For an SSO the local time of the ascending node is the single most consequential power decision a mission makes — worth more than a factor of two in annual energy, at zero mass cost.

本节要点
同样是 SSO，LTAN 从 10:30 换到 06:00（晨昏轨道），年均光照占空比从约 0.63 提升到 0.94 以上， 平均阴影时长从 36 分钟降到 6 分钟以内。这是一个不花任何重量代价的设计变量，做能耗研究时必须显式建模。

Between LTAN 07:00 and 08:00 the orbit crosses β*, and the annual profile changes character: from a mostly eclipse-free orbit with two short shadow seasons, to a permanently eclipsed one. Missions sited near that boundary are the ones whose energy models must not use an annual average.
§ 5

Inclination and the beta cycle

A non-sun-synchronous orbit's node regresses at a rate the Sun does not match, so the geometry beats against the year at a period set by inclination.

本节要点
非太阳同步轨道的 β 以"拍频"周期变化，周期长度由倾角决定（53° / 550 km 约为 66 天）。 能量可用性因此呈现准周期性，年平均值会严重掩盖最坏工况——论文里应报告分位数与最坏轨道圈，而不是均值。

§ 6

Walker shells share their energy over the year, never at the same moment

Every satellite in a Walker plane carries that plane's beta angle, so energy availability is a per-plane property. Whether that heterogeneity averages out over a year turns out to depend entirely on whether the shell is sun-synchronous.

本节要点
这是做星上算力/能耗调度最关键的一条，而且结论比直觉更细： 非太阳同步的 Walker 壳层（如 53°/550 km），所有轨道面的进动速率相同，只是相位不同 — 因此年累计能量几乎完全均匀（面间差异仅约 2%），但任一时刻各面的阴影时长差异极大。 而太阳同步 Walker（各面 LTAN 固定不同）的差异是永久性的、结构性的，年均占空比可以相差 40% 以上。 前者需要按时间调度，后者需要按轨道面分配任务。

§ 8

Model, validation, and what the assumptions cost

Numbers are only usable in a paper if the reader can see where they break. This section states the model, its verification against closed-form and almanac references, and the measured sensitivity to every discretionary choice.

Model

Circular orbits are propagated in mean elements with the secular J2 rates for the node, the argument of perigee and the mean anomaly, giving an exact nodal period and the correct nodal regression — the term that dominates illumination geometry over months. The Sun uses the Astronomical Almanac low-precision series (< 0.01° in longitude), including the inverse-square irradiance modulation of ±3.3 % between perihelion and aphelion.

Illumination is the unocculted fraction of the solar disc, computed as the circle–circle lens overlap of the apparent discs of the Sun and of the Earth as seen from the spacecraft, with the Earth's occulting radius taken as R⊕ + 90 km. Shadow entry and exit epochs are then refined by bisection on the continuous shadow function, which makes reported durations independent of the sampling grid.

Known limits

Orbits are circular and the elements are mean, so short-period J2 oscillations (a few kilometres) and drag decay are not modelled; both move the shadow boundary by well under a second per revolution but drag does change the altitude — and therefore β* — over a multi-year mission. Earth is a sphere for shadow purposes; oblateness slightly shortens eclipses at high latitudes. Albedo and Earth infrared input to the array are excluded, which is conservative for power and non-conservative for thermal analysis. Battery ageing, temperature-dependent cell efficiency and array shadowing by the bus are outside the scope of the illumination model.

Reproducing this

The full pipeline is three commands against a single dependency (NumPy):

python -m experiments.validate # verification suite, exits non-zero on failure python -m experiments.run_study # E1–E6, one simulated year each python -m experiments.run_profiles # E7, master curve and orbit profiles

Every figure on this page reads the JSON those commands write; nothing is transcribed by hand. Simulation parameters are recorded inside each result file alongside the values.
