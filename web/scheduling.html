How much compute does an orbit pay for?

The smallest defensible chain from orbital illumination to a power budget a scheduler can plan against — one citable model, one parameter table, the bounds a schedule has to respect, and the headroom it exists to capture.

Reference platform 2 m² array · 600 Wh · 45 W bus Traces 3 orbits · 10 s slots Epoch —

A constant payload draw has to survive the worst moment of every orbit, so it leaves generation on the table for the rest of it. The gap between that baseline and the scheduled optimum is not a modelling artefact — it is the thing an energy-aware scheduler is for, and it is worth quantifying before proposing one.

§ 1

The model to cite, and nothing more

For a scheduling paper, the power subsystem is an input, not a contribution. It needs to be standard, traceable, and about a paragraph long.

本节要点
调度论文里，电源模型是“输入”而不是“贡献”。用 SMAD 第十一章的标准链条，把轨道几何那一项换成逗度积分—— 一个公式、一张参数表、一条引用，就足够支撑你的调度结果了。再深会摄焦点，审稿人也不会要。
Pgen(t) = ν(t) · S(t) · A · ηcell · fpack · fdeg · ηppt · κ(t) ν(t) fractional illumination -- solar disc unocculted by the Earth S(t) instantaneous irradiance, S₀(AU/r☉)² κ(t) array cosine factor for the pointing architecture A, η cell area and efficiency; f terms are packing and end-of-life losses

The paragraph

Generated power follows the standard photovoltaic sizing chain of Wertz & Larson [SMAD, Ch. 11]. The orbital term ν(t) is the fraction of the solar disc unocculted by the Earth, computed from the circle–circle overlap of the apparent discs of the Sun and Earth as seen from the spacecraft, with the occulting radius taken as R⊕ + 90 km to account for the optically thick atmosphere [Montenbruck & Gill, §3.4]; shadow entry and exit epochs are refined by bisection so that reported durations are independent of the sampling grid. Irradiance uses the 1361 W/m² total solar irradiance of Kopp & Lean (2011) scaled by the inverse square of the instantaneous Sun–Earth distance. The orbit is propagated in mean elements with secular J2 rates, which is what governs the beta-angle drift over a planning horizon of weeks.

That is the whole model. It is enough to defend any energy number in a scheduling paper, and it is short enough to sit in a methods section without displacing the contribution.

§ 3

The baseline, the optimum, and the gap between them

Three numbers describe an orbit's compute budget: what a fixed draw survives, what a schedule free to vary achieves, and how far above either you can burst.

本节要点
常见的“轨道平均发电减户耗”其实不是估算错了，而是只有完美调度才能达到的上界。 恰恰相反：恒定功率基线因为要摋住每圈最坏时刻，在阳照区电池充满后只能弃电—— 两者之间+9% 到 +33% 的差距，就是能耗感知调度的收益空间。而且阴影越长，这个空间越大—— 晨昏轨道只有 +9%，普通 SSO 有 +33%。这可以直接做你论文的 motivation 图。

§ 4

The scheduling problem, in the form that stays linear

Battery dynamics with asymmetric charge and discharge efficiency look non-convex written naively. Splitting the flow into two non-negative variables keeps the whole thing an LP.

本节要点
直接写 b[k] = b[k-1] + Δ(…)⁺ 会引入非凝性。把充放电拆成两个非负变量 c[k]、d[k]， 约束全部线性，目标线性时是标准 LP；最优解处 c[k]·d[k]=0 自动成立（同时充放电只会白白损耗能量），不需要额外的互斥约束。
indices k = 1..K scheduling slots of length Δ given G[k] generated power in slot k (from the trace) P_h housekeeping draw B battery capacity, D depth-of-discharge limit η_c, η_d charge / discharge efficiency variables p[k] ≥ 0 payload power c[k] ≥ 0 power into the battery d[k] ≥ 0 power out of the battery b[k] stored energy at the end of slot k maximise Σk w[k] · p[k] · Δ subject to G[k] + d[k] = P_h + p[k] + c[k] power balance b[k] = b[k-1] + Δ( η_c c[k] - d[k]/η_d ) state of charge (1 - D)·B ≤ b[k] ≤ B depth-of-discharge limit 0 ≤ p[k] ≤ P_max accelerator ceiling b[K] ≥ b[0] no borrowing across the horizon

The terminal condition b[K] ≥ b[0] is what makes the result repeatable rather than a one-off raid on the battery; drop it and the optimiser will happily spend the whole reserve in the last orbit of the horizon. With it in place, the optimum of this LP over a whole number of revolutions is exactly the sustainable-power bound of §3 — which is a useful sanity check on any implementation.

Weights w[k] carry whatever the scheduler is really optimising: task value, deadline urgency, ground-contact windows, thermal derating. Non-linear utility in p[k] (diminishing returns from clock scaling, for instance) makes it a convex program rather than an LP, which is still tractable at these sizes — three orbits at 10 s slots is about 1 700 slots and 5 200 variables per satellite, solved by HiGHS in roughly ten seconds.

This LP is implemented as solarsim.schedule.optimal_schedule(), and it is what produces the “optimally scheduled” figures throughout this page. Two properties make it a useful reference point rather than just an example. Its optimum with w[k] = 1 lands within half a percent of orbit-average generation minus housekeeping in all three orbits, which confirms that the familiar back-of-envelope number is an upper bound reachable only by scheduling. And solving it with the periodicity constraint removed recovers the burst envelope of Figure 2, which is a cheap consistency check on any reimplementation.

The pattern: every one of these scales Pgen by a factor that is roughly constant across the schedules being compared. A scheduling result expressed as a ratio against a baseline is nearly invariant to them — which is the strongest argument for reporting ratios.

The one thing worth adding, if a reviewer pushes

Not more power physics — an independent cross-check of the eclipse geometry. GMAT (NASA, open source) and Orekit (Apache 2.0) both carry validated eclipse detectors. Reproducing the eclipse durations with either and reporting the agreement costs an afternoon and answers the “how do we know your energy input is right” question far more directly than any amount of added thermal modelling.
